About

Hi, I'm Frank Force. I'm a programmer with 25 years of professional experience shipping real-time interactive software. From AAA titles like DOOM, Starhawk, and Red Faction: Guerrilla to an award-winning independent catalog. I've held key roles at Certain Affinity, Rooster Teeth, Midway Games, Volition, and several other studios.

I created LittleJS, an open source HTML5 game engine with over 4,000 stars on GitHub, and ZzFX, a procedural sound synthesizer used in thousands of web games. I have deep expertise in JavaScript, WebGL, physics, procedural generation, and AI-assisted development. I won Best Illusion of the Year with my Dual Axis Illusion and have taken top prizes in multiple international game jams. Just for fun, I've written over 1,000 super tiny programs that do all sorts of cool stuff.

My company, Frank Force Games is located in Austin, Texas. We specialize in interactive software development including games, art, tools, promotional software, and educational resources.
